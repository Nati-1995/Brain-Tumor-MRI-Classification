The honest story

The original project reported 78.7% multiclass. Diagnosis revealed the cause wasn't the model. It was tangled preprocessing and premature early stopping. After fixing the pipeline and evaluating on clean stratified splits:

before: 78.7%  →  after: 94.75% ± 0.40%
verified over 3 independent random seeds.

Findings

What we learned

  • Preprocessing order matters more than architecture. CLAHE applied on top of ImageNet normalization was silently degrading the input. Fixing it moved the needle more than any model change.
  • The benchmark's own split was misleading. Re-splitting the data stratified and leakage-free exposed the model's true ceiling.
  • Meningioma is the genuine hard case. It confuses with glioma because they look similar on MRI, and Grad-CAM shows exactly when the model loses the lesion.
  • Confidence tracks correctness. On the missed cases the model reports low confidence and diffuse attention. It knows when it doesn't know.
